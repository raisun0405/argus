Answer

HIV-1 protease with indinavir (MK1)

1HSG

HIV-1 protease is a symmetric homodimer: each monomer brings its catalytic Asp25 to one shared active site. In 1HSG the drug indinavir (MK1) fills that site, clamped between the catalytic aspartates and the flexible flaps (Ile50).

    06Investigate

    Big questions, answered step by step.

    When a question needs several tools, Argus plans the steps, runs them in your browser, measures how the results relate in 3D, and writes an answer that cites every step.

    • Every residue it marks comes from a step, not from its memory.
    • Click a citation, or a step, to see it in 3D.
    • Pick Investigate from the + menu, or start a message with investigate:

      09Privacy

      No accounts. Nothing on a server.

      No accounts
      Open it and ask. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing stored on a server.
      Straight from your browser
      The science databases are free, keyless and called directly, so their data never passes through an Argus server.
      Keys stay at the edge
      Three edge functions hold the only keys: the analysis, Investigate's plan and write-up, and Co-fold.
      Links that keep secrets
      Answers are cached in your browser, and a share link carries its view in the part of the URL that never reaches a server.
